About Crafted Media

We make photographs that feel like being there.

Not just how a day looked, but how the room felt, who held your hand and what happened between the planned moments.

Our story

A camera is only the beginning.

Crafted Media is a Bengaluru-based photography and cinematography studio built around patient observation and meaningful storytelling. We work across weddings, personal celebrations, events and brand assignments throughout India and at destination events.

Our approach is personal without becoming intrusive, polished without losing honesty, and cinematic without turning real life into a performance. We prepare carefully, then leave enough room for the unexpected.

That balance lets people stay present while we preserve the moments they may have missed.
